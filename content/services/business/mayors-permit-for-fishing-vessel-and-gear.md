# Mayor's Permit for Fishing Vessel, Gear & Fishing Activities

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler) / Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C, G2B  
**Who May Avail:** Fisherfolks and Fishing Operators  

---

## Service Overview
This service regulates the fishing activities within the municipal waters of Baler. It involves the registration and licensing of fishing vessels (motorized and non-motorized) and fishing gears to ensure sustainable and legal fishing practices.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. Certificate of Registration and Fisherfolk ID (FishR)
2. Barangay Clearance
3. Clearance/Certification from the Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO)
4. Official Receipt of previous registration (for renewal)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Proceed to the MAO for inspection of the fishing vessel/gear and secure a clearance/endorsement.
2. Submit the MAO Clearance and other requirements to the BPLO for assessment.
3. Secure the Order of Payment.
4. Pay the required fees at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO).
5. Present the Official Receipt to the BPLO to claim the Mayor's Permit for Fishing Vessel/Gear.
