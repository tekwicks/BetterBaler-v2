# Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP)

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Tricycle Operators and Drivers operating in Baler, Aurora  

---

## Service Overview
The Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP) is the official document granting permission to operate a tricycle-for-hire within specified zones in the Municipality of Baler.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

| Requirement | Where to Secure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Tricycle Franchise Certificate | Franchise Regulatory Board (Legislative / SB Office) |
| Certificate of Registration (CR) & Latest Official Receipt (OR) | Land Transportation Office (LTO) |
| Barangay Clearance | Barangay where operator resides |
| TODA Certification | TODA President where associated |

---

## Fees to be Paid (Section 3X.09.c of Revenue Code 2019)

| Fee Item | Amount |
| :--- | :---: |
| Official Franchise Application Form / Franchise Certificate | ₱100.00 |
| Motorized Tricycle Operator's Permit (MTOP) Fee | ₱300.00 |
| MTOP Plate | ₱150.00 |
| MTOP ID | ₱50.00 |
| Annual Sticker | ₱25.00 |
| Official Fare Matrix ("Taripa") | ₱25.00 |
| Copy of Tricycles-for-Hire Ordinance | ₱25.00 |

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | File accomplished MTOP Application Form at BOSS Info Desk. | Verify, validate requirements, issue Order of Payment, and prepare MTOP documents. | None | 10 minutes | BPLO Administrative Aide |
| **2** | Pay fees at Window 6. | Issue Official Receipt based on Revenue Code schedule. | ₱675.00 Total | 5 minutes | MTO Revenue Collection Clerk |
| **3** | Claim MTOP, Sticker, Taripa, and Official Receipt at BOSS Info Desk. | Release MTOP permit, Taripa fare matrix card, sticker, and official receipt. | None | 5 minutes | BPLO Administrative Aide |

**Total Processing Time:** 20 Minutes
