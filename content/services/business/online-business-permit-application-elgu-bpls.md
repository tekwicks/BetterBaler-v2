# Online Business Permit Application (eLGU BPLS Portal)

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2B - Government to Business  
**Who May Avail:** Business Owners operating in Baler, Aurora  

---

## Service Overview
Business permit applications and annual renewals in Baler can be facilitated 100% online through the official eLGU Business Permit and Licensing System (`ebpls.baler.gov.ph`). This portal allows business owners to register, upload clearances, receive digital Tax Orders of Payment (TOP), pay online, and receive Mayor's Permits.

---

## Online Portal Access
- **Official Portal:** [ebpls.baler.gov.ph](https://ebpls.baler.gov.ph)

---

## Checklist of Requirements

### For New Business Applications:
1. DTI Registration (Single Proprietorship) / SEC Registration (Corporation/Partnership) / CDA (Cooperative)
2. Contract of Lease (if renting) or Land Title / Tax Declaration
3. Occupancy Permit (for newly constructed building)
4. Authorization Letter (if representative)

### For Business Permit Renewal:
1. Prior Year Mayor's Permit & Business Plate
2. BIR Income Tax Return (Quarterly/Annual) or Financial Statements
3. Local Regulatory Clearances (MENRO ESWM, Sanitary Permit, Fire Safety Inspection Certificate)

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Visit `ebpls.baler.gov.ph`, click "REGISTER", create account/login via Gmail, fill out Taxpayer & Business Information, and SUBMIT. | Verify completeness of basic requirements and data correctness in eLGU BPLS portal. | None | 5 minutes | BPLO / IT Officer |
| **2** | Wait for regulatory office endorsement online. | Regulatory offices (MENRO, MHO, BFP) endorse application and issue clearances online. | None | 5 minutes | MENRO / MHO / BFP Officers |
| **3** | Receive digital Tax Order of Payment (TOP) via email/portal. | MTO assesses taxes and generates Tax Order of Payment. | None | 5 minutes | MTO Revenue Collector |
| **4** | Click "PAYMENT", select online payment option, and submit. | Issue Official Receipt upon online payment confirmation. | Refer to Revenue Code 2019 | 5 minutes | MTO Cashier |
| **5** | Download or request delivery of Mayor's Permit & Plate. | Upload signed Mayor's Permit, Official Receipts, and clearances to eLGU BPLS; or deliver. | None | 5 minutes | BPLO Administrative Aide / IT Officer |

**Total Time:** 30 Minutes (Excluding waiting/payment confirmation time)
