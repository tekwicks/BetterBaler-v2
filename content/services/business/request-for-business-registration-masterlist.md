# Request for Business Registration Masterlist

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C, G2B, G2G  
**Who May Avail:** Students, Researchers, Business Organizations, Government Agencies  

---

## Service Overview
The BPLO provides certified true copies or electronic lists of registered businesses operating within Baler for research, benchmarking, or institutional purposes.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. Formal Letter of Request addressed to the Municipal Mayor (stating the purpose of the request)
2. Valid ID of the requesting party
3. Authorization Letter (if representing an organization or agency)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Submit the letter of request to the Mayor's Office for approval.
2. Once approved, present the approved letter to the BPLO.
3. Pay the required certification fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office.
4. Present the Official Receipt to the BPLO and claim the certified masterlist.
