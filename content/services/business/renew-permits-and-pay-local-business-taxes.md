# Application for Business Permit (Renewal Application)

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple to Complex (Depending on business nature)  
**Type of Transaction:** G2B - Government to Business  
**Who May Avail:** Business Owners operating in Baler, Aurora  

---

## Service Overview

The Mayor's Business Permit is required to be renewed annually by all businesses operating within the Municipality of Baler. Renewal ensures continued compliance with local ordinances, zoning, health, and safety regulations. Applications are processed through the Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS).

---

## Checklist of Requirements

### Basic Requirements
1. Prior year Mayor's Permit and Official Receipts
2. Sworn Declaration of Gross Sales/Receipts for the preceding year or Audited Financial Statements / ITR
3. Barangay Business Clearance (from the Barangay where the business is located)

### Regulatory Clearances (To be secured during the process or presented if updated)
1. Zoning / Locational Clearance (MPDO)
2. Sanitary Permit (MHO)
3. Solid Waste Management Certificate (MENRO)
4. Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (BFP)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Secure Renewal Application Form at the BOSS Info Desk and fill it out completely. | Provide form and assist with inquiries. | None | 5 minutes | BPLO Administrative Aide |
| **2** | Submit the accomplished form along with previous year's permit, receipts, and gross sales declaration. | Verify completeness of documents. If complete, receive and encode application. | None | 10 minutes | BPLO Assessor/Encoder |
| **3** | Proceed to respective regulatory desks (Zoning, Health, Environment, Fire) for assessment, verification, and clearances. | Assess documents, conduct inspections (if necessary), and issue clearances/endorsements. | Vary per office/nature of business | 1-2 hours | Respective Regulatory Officers |
| **4** | Return to BPLO Assessment Desk with complete clearances. | Compute Mayor's Permit Fee, Local Business Taxes, and other regulatory fees. Issue Order of Payment. | None | 10 minutes | BPLO Assessment Officer |
| **5** | Proceed to the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) cashier and pay the assessed amount. | Receive payment and issue Official Receipt (OR). | Based on Assessment | 10 minutes | MTO Revenue Collector |
| **6** | Present OR and claim the approved Mayor's Business Permit and Business Plate/Sticker. | Log details, attach OR, prepare permit for Mayor's signature, and release to the applicant. | None | 15 minutes | BPLO Releasing Officer / Municipal Mayor |

**Total Processing Time:** 2-3 Hours (Assuming all documentary requirements and clearances are complete and no major issues arise).
