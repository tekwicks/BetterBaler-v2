# Application for Occupational Permit

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler) & Municipal Health Office (MHO)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Employees working in commercial, industrial, or service establishments in Baler  

---

## Service Overview
The Occupational Permit (often tied with the Mayor's Working Permit and Health/Sanitary Card) is required for all employees to ensure they are fit to work and do not pose health risks, particularly in food and personal care establishments.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. Valid ID / Police Clearance / NBI Clearance
2. Medical Certificate / Lab Results (Urinalysis, Fecalysis, Chest X-ray) from the MHO or accredited clinics
3. 1x1 ID Picture
4. Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Submit lab results to the Municipal Health Office (MHO) for evaluation.
2. Secure the Health Certificate from the MHO.
3. Proceed to the BPLO and present the Health Certificate and other requirements.
4. Secure the Order of Payment.
5. Pay the Occupational Permit Fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO).
6. Present the Official Receipt to BPLO and claim the Occupational Permit/ID.
