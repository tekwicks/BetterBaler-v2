# Application for Business Permit (New Application)

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple to Complex (Depending on business nature)  
**Type of Transaction:** G2B - Government to Business  
**Who May Avail:** Business Owners intending to operate in Baler, Aurora  

---

## Service Overview

The Mayor's Business Permit is required for all businesses operating within the Municipality of Baler. It ensures that the enterprise complies with local ordinances, zoning, health, and safety regulations. Applications are processed through the Business One-Stop Shop (BOSS).

---

## Checklist of Requirements

### Basic Requirements
1. DTI Registration (Single Proprietorship) / SEC Registration (Corporation/Partnership) / CDA (Cooperative)
2. Contract of Lease (if renting) or Land Title / Tax Declaration (if owned)
3. Barangay Business Clearance (from the Barangay where the business is located)
4. Picture of Business Establishment (Exterior and Interior)

### Regulatory Clearances (To be secured during the process)
1. Zoning / Locational Clearance (MPDO)
2. Sanitary Permit (MHO)
3. Solid Waste Management Certificate (MENRO)
4. Fire Safety Inspection Certificate (BFP)
5. Building/Occupancy Permit (MEO)

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Secure Application Form at the BOSS Info Desk and fill it out completely. | Provide form and assist with inquiries. | None | 5 minutes | BPLO Administrative Aide |
| **2** | Submit the accomplished form along with basic requirements to the Receiving/Verification Officer. | Verify completeness of documents. If complete, receive and encode application. | None | 10 minutes | BPLO Assessor/Encoder |
| **3** | Proceed to respective regulatory desks (Zoning, Health, Environment, Engineering, Fire) for assessment and clearances. | Assess documents, conduct inspections (if necessary), and issue clearances/endorsements. | Vary per office/nature of business | 1-2 hours | Respective Regulatory Officers |
| **4** | Return to BPLO Assessment Desk with complete clearances. | Compute Mayor's Permit Fee, Local Business Taxes, and other regulatory fees. Issue Order of Payment. | None | 10 minutes | BPLO Assessment Officer |
| **5** | Proceed to the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) cashier and pay the assessed amount. | Receive payment and issue Official Receipt (OR). | Based on Assessment | 10 minutes | MTO Revenue Collector |
| **6** | Present OR and claim the approved Mayor's Business Permit and Business Plate/Sticker. | Log details, attach OR, prepare permit for Mayor's signature, and release to the applicant. | None | 15 minutes | BPLO Releasing Officer / Municipal Mayor |

**Total Processing Time:** 2-3 Hours (Assuming all documentary requirements and clearances are complete and no major site inspection issues arise).
