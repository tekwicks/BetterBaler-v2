# Public Market Stall Rental & Sealing of Weights & Measures

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler) / Economic Enterprise Management Office  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C, G2B  
**Who May Avail:** Vendors, Market Stallholders, Business Owners  

---

## Service Overview
This service covers the application for renting stalls at the Baler Public Market and Pasalubong Center. It also includes the mandatory registration, calibration, and sealing of instruments of weights and measures (weighing scales) used in businesses.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. Letter of Intent to Rent a Stall (addressed to the Mayor/Market Administrator)
2. Valid ID and Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
3. For Sealing of Weights: The weighing scale instrument and previous receipt of calibration (if applicable)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Submit the Letter of Intent or present the weighing scale to the BPLO / Market Administrator.
2. The office will assess availability (for stalls) or test/calibrate the instrument.
3. Secure the Order of Payment.
4. Pay the required rental fee or calibration fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO).
5. Present the Official Receipt to the respective office to finalize the lease contract or receive the calibration seal/certificate.
