# Application for Peddlers Permit (Ambulant Vendors)

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Ambulant vendors, street hawkers, and mobile merchants operating in Baler  

---

## Service Overview
A Peddler's Permit ensures that ambulant vendors are registered, occupy designated zones, and comply with basic health and sanitation standards (especially for food vendors).

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. Barangay Clearance (from the vendor's barangay of residence)
2. Health Certificate / Sanitary Permit (if selling food items)
3. 2x2 ID Picture
4. Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Submit the requirements to the BPLO Info Desk.
2. The BPLO evaluates the application and identifies the designated vending zone/route.
3. Secure Order of Payment from BPLO.
4. Pay the Peddler's Fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO).
5. Present the Official Receipt to BPLO and claim the Peddler's ID/Permit.
