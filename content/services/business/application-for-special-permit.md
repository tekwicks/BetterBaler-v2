# Application for Special Permit (Events, Shows & Filming)

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C, G2B  
**Who May Avail:** Event Organizers, Production Outfits, Associations, Individuals  

---

## Service Overview
A Special Permit from the Mayor's Office is required for holding temporary promotional sales, concerts, motorcades, parades, film showings, video shootings, and other similar activities in public places or private spaces intended for public access in Baler.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. Letter Request addressed to the Municipal Mayor indicating the details of the event (date, time, venue, nature of activity)
2. Endorsement/Clearance from the Barangay where the event will be held
3. Police Clearance / Security Plan (from Baler MPS) for large events
4. Contract of Lease/Consent (if the venue is privately owned)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Submit the Letter Request and requirements to the Mayor's Office for approval.
2. Once approved by the Mayor, proceed to the BPLO for assessment.
3. Secure the Order of Payment.
4. Pay the Special Permit Fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO).
5. Present the Official Receipt to the BPLO to claim the Special Permit.
