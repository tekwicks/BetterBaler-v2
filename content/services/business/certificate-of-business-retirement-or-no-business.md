# Request for Certificate of Business Retirement or No Business

**Office / Division:** Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C, G2B  
**Who May Avail:** Business Owners closing their business or individuals needing proof of no business  

---

## Service Overview
A Certificate of Business Retirement is issued to formally declare the closure or cessation of a business operation. A Certificate of No Business is issued to individuals as a requirement for scholarships, indigency, or other legal purposes.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

**For Business Retirement:**
1. Notarized Affidavit of Closure/Retirement
2. Original Mayor's Permit and Business Plate/Sticker
3. Gross Sales Declaration for the current year
4. Barangay Certificate of Closure

**For Certificate of No Business:**
1. Valid ID
2. Barangay Certification (stating the purpose)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Submit requirements to the BPLO Assessment Desk.
2. For business retirement, the BPLO and MTO will assess any remaining tax liabilities or deficiencies.
3. Pay the assessed tax deficiencies (if any) and the Certification Fee at the MTO Cashier.
4. Present the Official Receipt to the BPLO.
5. Claim the requested Certificate.
