# Application for Investment Incentive (Baler Investment Code)

**Office / Division:** Local Economic and Investment Promotions Office (LEIPO) / BPLO  
**Classification:** Highly Technical  
**Type of Transaction:** G2B - Government to Business  
**Who May Avail:** New and expanding enterprises in Baler  

---

## Service Overview
Under the Baler Investment Incentive Code, qualified enterprises (such as those in tourism, agri-business, IT/BPO, and green economy) may apply for local tax holidays and support services to foster economic growth and employment in the municipality.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. Letter of Intent addressed to the Municipal Mayor / Local Investment Board
2. Accomplished Application Form
3. Project Feasibility Study or Business Plan
4. SEC/DTI Registration and Articles of Incorporation
5. Environmental Compliance Certificate (ECC) or Certificate of Non-Coverage (CNC)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. Submit the Letter of Intent and complete documentary requirements to the LEIPO.
2. The LEIPO conducts initial evaluation and site inspection.
3. The Local Investment Board convenes to deliberate on the application.
4. If approved, a Certificate of Registration for Investment Incentives is issued.
5. The business may now avail of the tax incentives during the annual business permit renewal.
