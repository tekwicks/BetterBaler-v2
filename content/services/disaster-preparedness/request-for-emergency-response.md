# Emergency Response & Rescue Operations

**Office / Division:** Municipal Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (MDRRMO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen, G2G - Government to Government  
**Who May Avail:** General Public / Any person experiencing an emergency in Baler  

---

## Service Overview
MDRRMO Baler provides immediate 24/7 emergency response, medical evacuation, search and rescue, disaster assistance, and transport to healthcare facilities to preserve life and safety.

## Step-by-Step Emergency Dispatch Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Call MDRRMO Hotline **0920-594-1906**. | Answer hotline call immediately. | FREE | 1 minute | MDRRMO Operation & Warning Personnel |
| **2** | State caller name, exact location, nature of incident, time, and number of patients/victims. | Log incident details, location coordinates, caller contact, and victim condition. | FREE | 2 minutes | MDRRMO Operation & Warning Personnel |
| **3** | Wait at a safe location, assist victims if trained, or answer follow-up queries. | Organize rescue team, dispatch Emergency Response Team, ambulance, and medical kits. | FREE | 2 minutes | MDRRMO Operation & Warning Personnel |
| **4** | On-site response and transport. | Perform rescue, emergency triage, stabilization, and immediate transport to nearest hospital. | FREE | 10 minutes or more | MDRRMO Emergency Response Team |

**Total Time:** 15 Minutes (Immediate dispatch)  
**Fee:** FREE
