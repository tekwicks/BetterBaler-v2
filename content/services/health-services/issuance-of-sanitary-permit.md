# Issuance of Sanitary Permit

**Office / Unit:** Municipal Health Office (MHO - Sanitary Unit)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen, G2B - Government to Business  
**Who May Avail:** All business owners (Food and Non-Food establishments in Baler)  

---

## Service Overview
Any person or establishment wishing to operate a food or non-food business in Baler is required to secure a Sanitary Permit from the Municipal Health Office prior to starting operations, in compliance with the Sanitation Code of the Philippines.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

### Basic Requirements (Food and Non-Food):
1. Mayor's Business Permit (BPLO)
2. DTI / SEC / CDA Business Name Registration

### Specific Category Requirements:
- **Food Establishments / Ambulant Vendors:** Certificate of Water Potability, Photos of Sanitary Facilities (lavatories/CR), Food Handler's Training, Worker Health Certificates (Urinalysis, Fecalysis, Hepa-A, X-ray).
- **Water Refilling Stations:** Raw water physical-chemical analysis (initial), product water analysis (every 6 months), monthly microbiological analysis, worker health certificates.
- **Funeral Parlors:** License to practice (Embalmer), Sanitary Building Plan, worker health certificates.

## Fees & Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Submit complete requirements at MHO Sanitary Unit. | Evaluate requirements, process sanitary permit, and orient on health policies. Issue Tax Order of Payment. | None | 5 minutes | Sanitary Inspector |
| **2** | Pay corresponding fee at MHO Cashier. | Process payment and issue Official Receipt. | ₱300.00 | 3 minutes | Revenue Collection Clerk |
| **3** | Present Official Receipt and receive permit. | Verify receipt, record, and release signed Sanitary Permit. | None | 5 minutes | Sanitation Inspector |

**Total Time:** 13 Minutes (Excluding waiting time)  
**Fee:** ₱300.00
