# Issuance of Medical Certificate

**Office / Division:** Municipal Health Office (MHO - RHU Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Any person with health concerns requiring a medical certificate  

---

## Service Overview
A medical certificate is an official health document stating the current medical status of a patient. It is used for employment, school admission, PWD identification, sports clearance, and general administrative purposes.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

| Requirement | Where to Secure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Physical / Laboratory Examination (Chest X-ray, ECG, Drug Test, Urinalysis, Hematology as required) | DOH-Accredited Laboratories / Hospitals |
| Patient's Chart / Medical Record | Rural Health Unit (RHU Baler) |

---

## Fees to be Paid

- **PWD / Senior Citizens / Students:** ₱160.00
- **Regular Payment:** ₱200.00

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Proceed to the information desk. | Collect and verify all diagnostic requirements needed. | None | 3 minutes | Public Health Nurse / Midwife |
| **2** | Wait for the preparation of the certificate. | Preparation and filling out of the health/medical certificate. | None | 15 minutes | Municipal Health Officer |
| **3** | Proceed to the Rural Health Physician's room. | Medical assessment and signing of the certificate. | None | 3 minutes | Public Health Nurse / Midwife |
| **4** | Pay fee and receive medical certificate. | Recording, official receipt verification, and releasing of certificate. | ₱160.00 - ₱200.00 | 2 minutes | MTO Revenue Collector / PHN |

**Total Processing Time:** 23 Minutes
