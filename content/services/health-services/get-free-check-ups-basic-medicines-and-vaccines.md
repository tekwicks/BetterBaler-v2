# Outpatient Consultation & Medical Services

**Office / Division:** Municipal Health Office (MHO - RHU Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Any person with health concerns residing in or visiting Baler, Aurora  

---

## Service Overview
The Municipal Health Office of Baler provides free outpatient consultation, physical examination, health history taking, vital signs monitoring, and medical management for non-emergency cases.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

| Requirement | Where to Secure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Physical / Laboratory Examination Results (if available) | Diagnostic Laboratories / Hospitals |
| Patient’s Health Chart / Record | Rural Health Unit (RHU Baler) |

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Proceed to the information desk. | **Patient Assessment:**<br>• Physical examination<br>• History taking<br>• Vital signs monitoring | None | 3 minutes | Public Health Nurse (PHN) / Rural Health Midwife (RHM) |
| **2** | Wait for the patient's name to be called. | **Consultation & Management:**<br>• Physician evaluation<br>• Diagnosis & prescription issuance | None | 15 minutes | Municipal Health Officer (MHO) |
| **3** | Proceed to Laboratory or Supply Office if needed. | **Follow-up & Dispensing:**<br>• Laboratory procedure (upon doctor request)<br>• Dispensing of prescribed medicine<br>• Follow-up checkup scheduling | None | 2 minutes | Public Health Nurse (PHN) / Rural Health Midwife (RHM) |

**Total Time:** 20 Minutes (Excluding waiting time)  
**Total Fees:** FREE
