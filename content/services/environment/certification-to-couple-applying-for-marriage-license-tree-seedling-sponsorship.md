# Issuance of Certification to Couple Applying for Marriage License

**Office / Division:** Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C – Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Couple applying for Marriage License  

---

## Service Overview
It is a requirement for a couple applying for marriage license to sponsor seedlings of forest/fruit bearing trees and to be submitted at the MENRO office.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. **Forest/Fruit Bearing Trees**

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Submit the required number/pieces of forest or fruit bearing trees.**
   * The Administrative Aide will prepare the certification. (2 minutes)
   * The MENRO will approve the Certification. (1 minute)
   * *No fees required.*
2. **Claim the Certificate.**
   * The Administrative Aide will issue the Certification. (2 minutes)
   * *No fees required.*
