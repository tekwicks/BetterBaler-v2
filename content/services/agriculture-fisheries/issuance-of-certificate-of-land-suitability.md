# Issuance of Certificate of Land Suitability

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Highly Technical  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** General Public  

---

## Service Overview
Ocular inspection and land suitability certification for agricultural development, NIA transactions, or land transfer.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1.  Letter of Request for Land Suitability addressed to the Municipal Agriculturist
2.  Certified True Copy of OCT/TCT
3.  Certified True Copy of Absolute Deed of Sale (if the TCT/OCT is not yet transferred to the present owner)
4.  Certified True Copy of Tax Clearance
5.  Photocopy of Subdivision Plan; a photocopy of Vicinity/location map
6.  Certification from National Irrigation Administration (NIA)
7.  Picture of the Site

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Submit Request Letter and documentary requirements.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will receive and review the necessary documents.
    *   A schedule for an ocular inspection will be set. (10 minutes)
    *   *No fees for this step.*
2.  **Ocular Inspection.**
    *   Present the applied agricultural land to the inspectors.
    *   The ATs and Municipal Agriculturist will conduct the ocular inspection.
    *   If favorable, they will advise the landowner to pay the certification fee at the Municipal Treasurer's Office. (30 minutes)
3.  **Pay Fees and charges.**
    *   Proceed to the MTO Revenue Collection Clerk.
    *   Pay the Certification Fee of **P100.00**.
    *   An Official Receipt will be issued. (5 minutes)
4.  **Claim Certificate.**
    *   Present the Official Receipt to the MAO.
    *   The ATs will issue the Certificate of Land Suitability. (5 minutes)
