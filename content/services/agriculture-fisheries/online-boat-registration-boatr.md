# Online Boat Registration (BoatR)

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Fisher folks  

---

## Service Overview
Registration of municipal fishing boats with a gross tonnage capacity of 3 and below, issuing Motorized Vessel Registration (MVR) numbers.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1.  FishR Number (Secure from Municipal Agriculture Office)
2.  BoatR Form
3.  *Note: Eligible boats are those with gross metric tonnage capacity of 3 and below.*

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Fill-up BoatR Form.**
    *   Seek technical assistance from the MAO regarding the following data:
        1. Actual measurement of the boat.
        2. Photo record of the boat and boat owner.
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will review the completion of information and encode the profile information from the filled-up form to the BoatR website. (15 minutes)
    *   *No fees required.*
2.  **Claim Motorized Vessel Registration (MVR) Number.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists will issue the Motorized Vessel Registration (MVR) Number. (2 minutes)
    *   *No fees required.*
