# Availment of Planting Materials

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Farmers  

---

## Service Overview
Distribution of high-value vegetable seeds, rice/corn seeds, and fruit-bearing tree seedlings to support local farmers.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1.  FA/IA/RBO Farmer Profiles (Secure from Municipal Agriculture Office)
2.  Request Slip
3.  *Note: Requesting party must have a suitable planting area.*

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Submit Request Slip.**
    *   Submit the Request Slip for the availment of planting materials.
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will receive the request and set a schedule for farm site validation.
    *   If favorable, the requestee will be included among the beneficiaries. (10 minutes)
    *   *No fees required.*
