# Availment of Farm Machineries and Fishing Paraphernalia

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Farmers, Fisher folks, Associations/Cooperatives  

---

## Service Overview
Processing of requests for agricultural machinery (from DA RFO 3) and fishing paraphernalia/gear (from BFAR 3 / PFARO) to support local agricultural and coastal livelihoods.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

**For Farm Machineries (from DA RFO 3):**
1.  FA/IA/RBO Farmer Profiles (Secure from MAO)
2.  SEC/CDA/DOLE certificate of registration and good standing
3.  DA/CSO Accreditation
4.  Letter of Intent and a Board Resolution

**For Fishing Paraphernalia (from BFAR 3 and PFARO):**
1.  SEC/CDA/DOLE certificate of registration and good standing
2.  FishR Number
3.  Endorsement letter from Fisherfolks' Association / MFARMC / Barangay Captain

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

**For Farm Machineries:**
1.  **Submit the Letter of Intent and other supporting documents.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will review the completion of requirements. (5 minutes)
    *   The Municipal Agriculturist will request an Endorsement from the Provincial Agriculturist and Municipal Mayor. (1 day)
    *   The MAO will send the request for the availment of Farm Machineries to DA RFO III. Upon approval, the MAO will notify the requesting farmer/group. (5 minutes)
    *   *No fees required.*

**For Fishing Paraphernalia:**
1.  **Submit Endorsement letter and supporting documents.**
    *   The ATs will receive the Endorsement Letter. (5 minutes)
    *   The Municipal Agriculturist will validate the requester's FishR Number. (3 minutes)
    *   The MAO will send the request for the availment of interventions from BFAR 3 and PFARO. Upon approval, the MAO will notify the requesting fisherfolk/group. (1 day)
    *   *No fees required.*
