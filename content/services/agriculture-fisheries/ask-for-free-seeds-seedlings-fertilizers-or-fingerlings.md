# Provision of Technical Assistance on Rice, Corn, High Value Crops, Livestock and Fishery Production

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Highly Technical  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Farmers, Fisherfolks  

---

## Service Overview
The Municipal Agriculture Office provides technical guidance, field consultations, and farming advice on rice, corn, high-value crops, livestock, and aquaculture.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
*   Personal Appearance or Visit to MAO's Office

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Sign in the Visitor's/Clientele's Logbook.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will interview you regarding your needs and concerns. (10 minutes)
    *   You will be referred to the appropriate Program Coordinator based on your concern. (5 minutes)
    *   An immediate schedule for farm visits will be set if necessary. (2 minutes)
    *   *No fees required.*
