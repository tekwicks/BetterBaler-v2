# Online Fisherfolks Registration (FishR)

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Fisher folks  

---

## Service Overview
National fisherfolk database enrollment and assignment of unique FishR numbers.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1.  FishR Form (Hard Copy) - Secure from the Municipal Agriculture Office

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Submit duly filled FishR Form.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will review the completion of information.
    *   Profile information from the filled-up form will be encoded to the FishR website.
    *   A mug shot photo of the fisherfolk will be taken.
    *   Processing time: 15 minutes.
    *   *No fees required.*
2.  **Claim FishR Number.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists will issue the FishR Number to the fisherfolk. (2 minutes)
    *   *No fees required.*
