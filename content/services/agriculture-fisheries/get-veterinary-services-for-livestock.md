# Availment of Veterinary Health Services and Genetic Improvements

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Highly Technical  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Livestock Raiser  

---

## Service Overview
Provision of livestock health care, vitamin supplementation, deworming, vaccination, and genetic improvement services such as artificial insemination.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1.  Request Slip (Secure from Municipal Agriculture Office)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Submit Request Slip.**
    *   Submit the Request Slip for the availment of veterinary health services and genetic improvements to the MAO.
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will receive the request and set a schedule for a farm visit.
    *   They will conduct the necessary veterinary health services (e.g., vitamin supplementation, deworming) or genetic improvement services (e.g., artificial insemination).
    *   Processing time: 10 minutes (for the request and scheduling).
    *   *No fees required.*
