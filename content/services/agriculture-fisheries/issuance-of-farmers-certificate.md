# Issuance of Farmers Certificate

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Farmers  

---

## Service Overview
Verification of registered farmer status from the RSBSA general masterlist for loans, assistance, and other agricultural transactions.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1.  RSBSA Registration

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Submit RSBSA Registration.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will verify and validate your name from the General Masterlist. (10 minutes)
    *   A Certification will be prepared by the ATs or the Municipal Agriculturist. (5 minutes)
    *   *No fees for this step.*
2.  **Pay Fees and Charges.**
    *   Proceed to the Municipal Treasurer's Office (MTO) Revenue Collection Clerk.
    *   Pay the Certification fee of **P100.00**.
    *   An Official Receipt will be issued. (3 minutes)
3.  **Claim MAO's Certification.**
    *   Present the Official Receipt to the Municipal Agriculture Office.
    *   The Agricultural Technologists will issue the Certification. (2 minutes)
