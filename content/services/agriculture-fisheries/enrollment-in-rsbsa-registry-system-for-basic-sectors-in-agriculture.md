# Enrollment in Registry System for Basic Sector in Agriculture (RSBSA)

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Farmers, Fisherfolks  

---

## Service Overview
Registration of farmers, farm workers, and fisherfolk to the national registry to access government subsidies, interventions, and crop insurance.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

**General Requirements:**
1.  Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture (RSBSA) Enrollment Form
2.  2 x 2 ID picture
3.  Photocopy of Government Issued ID

**If Landowner or Heir:**
*   Photocopy of Land Title or Tax Declaration (Secure from Land Registration Authority or Municipal Assessor's Office)

**If Tenant or Farm Administrator:**
*   Barangay Certification of Farm Location
*   Authorization from Land owner

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Submit all documentary requirements.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) will review the documents required for RSBSA Registration. (10 minutes)
    *   The ATs will assist in the filing up of the RSBSA Enrollment Form. (5 minutes)
    *   *No fees required.*
