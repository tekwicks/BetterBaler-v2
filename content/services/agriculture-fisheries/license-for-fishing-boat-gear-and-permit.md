# Issuance of License for Fishing Boat and Gear and Permit for Fishing Activity

**Office / Division:** Municipal Agriculture Office (MAO) / Business Permit and Licensing Office (BPLO)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Fisher folks  

---

## Service Overview
Processing of MAO non-involvement certification and permit for commercial/municipal fishing vessels and gear.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1.  FishR Numbers of Boat Owner, Boat Captain and Tripulants (Secure from Municipal Agriculture Office)
2.  Barangay Clearance
3.  Community Tax Certificate or Cedula
4.  MAO's certification of non-involvement of boat to any illegal fishing activity

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1.  **Submit documentary requirements to MAO.**
    *   The Agricultural Technologists (ATs) and Municipal Agriculturist will receive and review the completion of the necessary documents.
    *   They will issue the MAO's certification of non-involvement of the boat to any illegal fishing activity. (10 minutes)
    *   *No fees for this step.*
2.  **Present Requirements to BOSS Info Desk (BPLO).**
    *   The BPLO Administrative Aide IV will issue the Tax Order of Payment and prepare the Permit for Fishing Vessels and Fishing Activity. (15 minutes)
3.  **Pay Fees and Charges.**
    *   Proceed to the MTO Revenue Collection Clerk.
    *   Pay the assessed fees (Base fee P100.00, varies by vessel type).
    *   An Official Receipt will be issued. (5 minutes)
4.  **Claim Permit.**
    *   Return to the BPLO Administrative Aide IV.
    *   Claim the Permit for Fishing Vessels and Fishing Activity.
    *   Furnish a copy to the MAO's Office. (5 minutes)
