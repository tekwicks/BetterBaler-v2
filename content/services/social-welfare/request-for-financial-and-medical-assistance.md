# Request for Financial and Medical Assistance

**Office / Division:** Office of the Municipal Mayor & MSWDO Baler  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Indigent residents of Baler with health, medical, or funeral/burial needs  

---

## Service Overview
The Office of the Municipal Mayor offers financial assistance to indigent individuals and families experiencing illness, hospitalization, or funeral/burial expenses.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

### For Medical Assistance:
1. Medical Certificate / Medical Abstract / Doctor's Prescription (from Hospital)
2. Barangay Certificate of Low Income or Indigency
3. Photocopy of Valid ID with 3 Specimen Signatures of Beneficiary
4. Duly filled-up MSWDO Social Case Intake Study Form

### For Burial / Funeral Assistance:
1. Death Certificate / Funeral Contract (from MCR / Funeral Home)
2. Barangay Certificate of Low Income or Indigency
3. Photocopy of Valid ID with 3 Specimen Signatures of Beneficiary
4. Duly filled-up MSWDO Social Case Intake Form

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Present required documents at Mayor's Office / MSWDO. | Receive documents, conduct interview, and review eligibility. | FREE | 5 minutes | Administrative Aide / Executive Secretary |
| **2** | Wait for evaluation. | Delegate assistance amount based on illness severity / medical need. Mayor signs approval. | FREE | 2 minutes | Municipal Mayor / Executive Secretary |
| **3** | Assistance processing. | Prepare voucher and financial assistance check/cash release. | FREE | 3-5 days | Administrative Aide / Executive Secretary |
| **4** | Return to Mayor's Office for release. | Release financial check/cash to requesting beneficiary. | FREE | 3 minutes | Administrative Aide / Executive Secretary |

**Total Time:** 5 Days and 10 Minutes  
**Fee:** FREE
