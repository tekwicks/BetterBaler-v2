# Application for Marriage License (AFML)

**Office / Division:** Municipal Civil Registrar's Office (MCRO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Contracting parties where either bride or groom habitually resides in Baler  

---

## Service Overview
The Local Civil Registrar issues a marriage license after completion of application requirements and mandatory 10-day posting period pursuant to Article 9 of the Family Code of the Philippines.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

| Requirement | Where to Secure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Certificate of Live Birth (COLB) or Baptismal Certificate | MCRO / PSA / Church |
| Pre-Marriage Orientation & Counseling (PMOC) Certificate | MSWDO / MHO |
| Tree Seedling Sponsorship Certificate | MENRO Baler |
| CENOMAR (Certificate of No Marriage) | PSA |
| Community Tax Certificate (Cedula) & Valid IDs | Barangay / MTO / Government |
| Parental Consent (ages 18-21) / Parental Advice (ages 21-25) | MCRO Form |

---

## Fees to be Paid

- **Church Wedding License Fee:** ₱1,500.00
- **Civil Wedding License Fee:** ₱2,500.00

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Inquire at MCR frontline desk. | Interview applicants and check eligibility. | None | 2 minutes | MCR Frontline Staff |
| **2** | Present complete requirements. | Verify documents and encode marriage license application. | None | 20 minutes | Administrative Aide / MCR |
| **3** | Review and sign AFML. | Verify signatures on the application form. | None | 5 minutes | MCR |
| **4** | Pay license fee at MTO Cashier. | Issue Official Receipt and advise 10-day posting period. | ₱1,500 - ₱2,500 | 5 minutes | MTO Revenue Collector |
| **5** | Return after 10-day posting period to claim. | MCR approves and releases Marriage License. | None | 2 minutes | MCR |

**Total Time:** 44 Minutes (Excluding 10-day statutory posting period)
