# Request for Security Paper (SECPA) from PSA via BREQS

**Office / Division:** Municipal Civil Registrar's Office (MCRO Baler - BREQS Outlet)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** Any citizen requesting official PSA-issued civil registry documents  

---

## Service Overview
Batch Request Query System (BREQS) allows citizens of Baler to apply for official PSA Security Paper (SECPA) copies of Birth, Marriage, Death certificates, and Certificates of No Marriage Record (CENOMAR) directly at Baler Municipal Hall without traveling to PSA provincial or regional centers.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

| Requirement | Where to Secure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Duly accomplished PSA Request Form | MCRO Baler Frontline |
| Valid Government Issued ID of Requesting Party | SSS / UMID / Driver's License / Passport / Voter ID |
| Authorization Letter & ID (if requesting for non-immediate family) | Document Owner |

---

## Fees to be Paid

- **LGU Service Fee:** ₱50.00 as per Revised Revenue Code of Baler
- *(Plus standard PSA document fee paid upon request)*

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Inquire and present request at frontline. | Interview client and check details on PSA form. | None | 2 minutes | BREQS Officer / MCR |
| **2** | Fill up prescribed PSA form. | Prepare and encode PSA electronic request. | None | 5 minutes | BREQS User Staff |
| **3** | Pay service fee at MTO Window. | Issue Official Receipt and advise release date (5 working days). | ₱50.00 | 5 minutes | MTO Revenue Collector |
| **4** | Transmit e-request to PSA Serbilis. | Staff emails civil events to PSA via BREQS portal. | None | 5 minutes | BREQS IT Officer |
| **5** | Claim SECPA document on scheduled date. | Verify receipt and release official PSA SECPA copy. | None | 5 minutes | BREQS Officer |

**Total Time:** 22 Minutes (Excluding 5-day PSA transmission and print time)
