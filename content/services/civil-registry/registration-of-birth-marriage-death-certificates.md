# Registration of Birth, Marriage, and Death Certificates (From Institutions)

**Office / Division:** Municipal Civil Registrar's Office (MCRO Baler)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2C - Government to Citizen  
**Who May Avail:** All citizens reporting vital events occurring in Baler institutions  

---

## Service Overview
Registration of vital events (Birth, Marriage, Death) occurring in hospitals, clinics, churches, or funeral parlors in Baler. Timely registration ensures official legal entry into the Civil Registry and national PSA database.

---

## Checklist of Requirements

| Requirement | Where to Secure |
| :--- | :--- |
| Municipal Form 102 (Certificate of Live Birth - 4 originals) | Hospital / Clinic |
| Municipal Form 97 (Certificate of Marriage - 4 originals) | Church / Solemnizing Officer |
| Municipal Form 103 (Certificate of Death - 4 originals) | Hospital / Funeral Service |
| Municipal Form 103A (Certificate of Fetal Death - 4 originals) | Hospital / Clinic |

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure

| Step | Client Steps | Agency Action | Fees to be Paid | Processing Time | Person Responsible |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **1** | Submit duly accomplished municipal forms to MCR frontline. | Review and verify contents and completeness of civil registry documents. | FREE | 10 minutes | Frontline Staff / Administrative Aide III, IV, VI / MCR |
| **2** | Receive registered copy and sign logbook. | Register document in Civil Registry book, assign registry number, and release official copy. | FREE | 5 minutes | Frontline Staff / MCR |

**Total Time:** 15 Minutes  
**Fee:** FREE
