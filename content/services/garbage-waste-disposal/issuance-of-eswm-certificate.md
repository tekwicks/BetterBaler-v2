# Issuance of ESWM Certificate

**Office / Division:** Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO)  
**Classification:** Simple  
**Type of Transaction:** G2B – Government to Business  
**Who May Avail:** Business Owners  

---

## Service Overview
There shall be collected an Ecological Solid Waste Management (ESWM) clearance fee for the issuance of clearance/certificate to all commercial, industrial and institutional establishments and agricultural business.

---

## Checklist of Requirements
1. **Unified Application Form (Business)** (Secure from Business Permit and Licensing Office)
2. **Official Receipt** (Secure from Municipal Treasurer's Office)

---

## Step-by-Step Procedure
1. **Submit requirements.**
   * The Administrative Aide will prepare the ESWM certificate after the client has been oriented about Ecological Solid Waste Management. (2 minutes)
   * The MENRO will approve the ESWM Certificate. (1 minute)
   * *Fee:* P200.00 for Certification
2. **Claim ESWM Certificate.**
   * The Administrative Aide will issue the ESWM Certificate. (2 minutes)
   * *No fees for this step.*
